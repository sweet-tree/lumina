# Lumina - Consciousness Practice App with Plant Teacher Guide

## Product Vision (Updated)

**Lumina is a mobile-first consciousness practice app where users sit in digital "dieta" with an AI Plant Teacher named Lumina. Through daily check-ins and breath work, users discover their unique consciousness patterns, collect cards that reveal their journey, and learn to navigate their inner terrain—combining ancient shamanic wisdom with modern AI.**

## Core Experience: Day 1

### The Breathing Card (First Check-In Only)

**Purpose:** Hook user immediately, teach embodied practice, differentiate from all other apps

**Flow:**

1. **Open app**
   - Screen mostly dark
   - Lumina's greeting appears (text)
   - Card materializes, glowing softly

2. **Card is breathing**
   - Visible pulse (expansion/contraction)
   - Alive, not static
   - Text on card: "Tap to breathe together"

3. **User taps card**
   - Breathing cycle begins
   - **Inhale (4 sec):** Card expands, text: "in..."
   - **Exhale (6 sec):** Card contracts, text: "out..."
   - **3 cycles total** (~30 seconds)

4. **After 3rd breath**
   - Card holds steady
   - Text changes: "Now... what's here?"
   - Input field appears below card

5. **User types/speaks**
   - Open field (no categories, no labels)
   - Natural expression (5 words or 5 minutes)
   - Lumina receives their truth

6. **AI reflection**
   - RAG-powered wisdom (Buddhist, Vedic, shamanic sources)
   - 2-4 sentences
   - Specific to their words + emerging pattern

7. **Card flips over**
   - Visual reveal animation
   - Shows their **Common Card** (from fixed library)
   - Card title: "Body Tension" / "Energy Flow" / etc.
   - Brief wisdom on back

8. **First seed planted**
   - Visual: seed appears in garden/soil
   - Text: "First seed planted. 29 days remaining."
   - Progress indicator visible

**Total time:** ~90 seconds (60 sec breath + 30 sec check-in)

**Emotional arc:** Calm → Curious → Seen → Committed

## Technical Architecture

### AI System (RAG-Powered)

**Knowledge base:**
- Buddhist psychology (5 Hindrances, 7 Factors of Awakening)
- Vedic wisdom (gunas, koshas, chakras)
- Leela teachings (72 consciousness squares)
- Shamanic concepts (plant medicine, ceremony)
- Modern psychology (polyvagal, attachment, shadow work)
- Dzogchen texts (Tibetan Buddhism)

**AI flow:**
1. User check-in received
2. Semantic search across knowledge base
3. Pattern detection (user's history)
4. Response generation (contextual, wise)
5. Card selection (best match)
6. Synthesis creation (weekly/monthly)

**Key capabilities:**
- Contextual awareness (knows user's patterns)
- Teaching moments (when to educate vs. reflect)
- Prediction (anticipates next state)
- Personalization (adapts to user)

---

### Card Selection Logic

**Inputs:**
1. User's specific words (semantic analysis)
2. User's pattern history (loops, doorways)
3. Current state indicators (body/energy/mind)
4. Leela wisdom mapping (consciousness squares)
5. Time/context (morning vs. evening)

**Process:**
1. Parse user input
2. Identify primary state (Body Tension, Energy Flow, etc.)
3. Check for pattern match (is this their loop?)
4. Map to Leela square (traditional teaching)
5. Select card from library (best fit)
6. Generate reflection (RAG wisdom)

**Output:**
- Common Card (90% of time)
- Rare Card trigger (weekly patterns)
- Epic Card trigger (monthly synthesis)

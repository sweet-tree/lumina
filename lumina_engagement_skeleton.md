# Lumina - Engagement Skeleton

## Core Philosophy
- Dzogchen: "Short moments, many times" - present moment only
- Plant Teacher (Lumina) guides through 30-day dieta
- No categories/labels - just "What's here right now?"
- Teaching happens in reflection (evening/weekly), not during practice

---

## The Voice: Lumina (Plant Teacher)
- Not human elder, not clinical AI
- Plant consciousness - mystical, shamanic, neither-male-nor-female, ancient, patient
- Like ayahuasca speaks, like trees speak in ceremony

**First Words:**
"You've been looking for something. That's why you're here. I can show you what you're looking for, but only if you show me what's true. What's here right now?"

---

## Check-In Mechanics

### During Day (Present Moment)
1. Open app
2. "What's here right now?"
3. Open field - type or speak (naturally, no forced depth)
4. AI reflection (RAG spiritual wisdom)
5. Card flips over (visual reveal)
6. Done

**Depth:** 
- User responds naturally (5 words or 5 minutes)
- No "go deeper" buttons
- No forced structure
- Whatever arises, arises

### Evening/Weekly (Post-Meditation Teaching)
- AI synthesis shows patterns
- Teaches: "Notice how body tension preceded energy depletion?"
- Suggests: "Tomorrow, when tension arises, what else is present?"

---

## Card Rarity System (RPG-Inspired)

### Common Cards (Daily Check-Ins)
- Fixed library (~50-100 cards)
- AI selects best match from library
- Quick, consistent, recognizable
- Examples: "Body Tension", "Energy Flow", "Mind Fog"

### Rare Cards (Weekly Synthesis)
- Dynamically generated
- Based on user's week's pattern
- Named patterns: "The Monday Morning Spiral"
- Custom artwork/description
- "Earned" insight

### Epic Cards (Monthly Synthesis)
- Fully unique to user
- Shows consciousness map/terrain
- Major pattern revelations
- Example: "Your Doorway: Awareness"
- Collectible, shareable

### Legendary Cards (Milestones)
- Breaking major loops
- 30-day streak completion
- Deep transformations
- Special occasions

---

## Card Selection Logic (Dynamic)

AI determines card based on:
1. **User's words** (specific language used)
2. **User's pattern** (AI knows their loops/doorways)
3. **Leela wisdom** (maps to consciousness square/teaching)
4. **= Unique card for this moment**
